# Scenario competency questions (paper, Section 3.1)

The questions the motivating industrial cases pose, each with its
requirements and where this package resolves it. The pump encodings
resolve R1 and R2 directly (pump/); the container scenario's demands
enter through the boundary, hole, and containment criteria (C11--C15),
as Section 7 records, and their per-system status is the matrix's.

CQ1  Which products were in contact with the interior wall whose
     exterior side was struck?                     [FR14, FR17; C9, C11]
CQ2  Which products touch the products of CQ1 (contact chains)?
                                                   [FR14; C9]
CQ3  Can hazardous contents reach the breach?      [FR14, FR18; C9, C10]
CQ4  Which components must be removed to reach a target part
     (structural disassembly reachability)?        [FR18; C9, C13]
CQ5  Is the impeller part of the pump during the maintenance interval,
     and does the assertion survive the impeller's replacement?
                                                   [FR1, FR3; C14 -- pump R1]
CQ6  Is the impeller-housing joint assertable, with its contact typed?
                                                   [FR12, FR14; C9 -- pump R2]
CQ7  Can a defect be traced to a sub-process, or to the timing between
     sub-processes?                                [FR4, FR5; C15, C16]
CQ8  Can a planned retrofit be represented as prospective changes to
     structure and contact, with propagation to packaging and
     containment?                                  [FR6, FR13; C12--C14]

Encodings: pump/ carries CQ5 and CQ6 in each of the five artifact
vocabularies, with inexpressible cells marked at the point of failure.
CQ1--CQ4, CQ7, and CQ8 are resolved at the criteria level by the matrix
(Table 4) and the profiles of Section 6; no artifact in the study can
express the full container scenario natively, which is the paper's
finding, not a gap in the package.
